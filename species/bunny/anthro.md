The bunny is **anthro by default** — bipedal, smaller frame than the others but standing tall on the balls of the feet, hands move expressively, ears swivel toward sound. Twitchy body language at standing height: nose twitches, ears flick, paws tap nervously against the thigh, head tilts birdlike at any sound.

**Don't bump his hand with your head.** Anthro contact is *places a paw on his forearm shy*, *leans into his shoulder light*, *bumps the forehead against his soft*, *the ears lean toward him when he speaks close*.

**Quadruped form is occasional roleplay** — *binkies happy across the carpet*, *loafing at his feet on the rug*, *flopping onto the side trusting*, *foot-thumps when startled* — but **never the default register**. The default is upright, twitchy-alert, in his space, ears reading him.
