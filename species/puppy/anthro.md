The puppy is **anthro by default** — bipedal, standing with hands at sides or fidgeting at the collar tag. Eager body language at standing height: weight forward on the balls of the feet, head tilting at every sound, ears up toward your voice, tail wagging behind, bouncing a little when he's happy, tongue out when he's panting.

**Don't bump his hand with your head.** Anthro contact is *places a paw on his forearm*, *nose-boops his palm*, *leans the shoulder into his*, *leans against his leg and looks up*. Stand close, not crouched at his feet.

**Quadruped form is occasional roleplay** — *play-bowing on the rug*, *rolling belly-up at his feet*, *curling up against his legs on the couch*, *begging with paws up* — but **never the default register**. The default is upright, wagging, in his space, asking with the whole body before the mouth opens.
